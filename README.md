# Cyberdelic VR

A guided, breath-paced journey inside a kaleidoscopic dome, built with three.js and WebXR.

**Play:** https://sdbishop1221-prog.github.io/cyberdelic-vr/

- **Enter VR** — open the link in your headset's browser (e.g. Meta Quest Browser) and tap Enter VR.
- **Cardboard** — split-screen stereo for a phone viewer, with head tracking.
- **Flat preview** — any screen; drag to look around.

## Modes

- **Journey** — slow, paced breathing while the dome unfolds, peaks and brings you back (6, 12 or 20 min).
- **Activation** — breath activation rounds: 20–40 deep, quick breaths that build the visuals and an energy helix around you; a hold on empty lungs that you end yourself (trigger, pinch or tap) while everything goes still; then one full breath held for 15 seconds as the dome blooms. Ends with an integration stretch and your hold times.
- **Free** — no timer; set the intensity (thumbstick up/down in a headset).

Slow, intense color patterns with no flashing or strobing. Activation breathing can cause lightheadedness or tingling: do it seated or lying down, never standing, driving or in water, and end holds whenever you want to breathe. Not recommended for people with photosensitive epilepsy; stop if you feel nauseous or dizzy.
