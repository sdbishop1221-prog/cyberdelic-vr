cyberdelic/vr

A breath-driven journey inside a kaleidoscopic dome. Visuals and sound respond to every breath. Best seated, with headphones.

ModeJourney

Slow breathing while the dome unfolds, peaks and brings you back down.

Length12 min

Rounds of deep, quick breaths build the visuals up. Then you hold on empty lungs while everything goes still, ending the hold whenever you need to breathe. One full breath held for 15 seconds makes the dome bloom.

Rounds3
Breaths per round30
Breath speed3.2 s per breath
Hold limit90 s

This breathing can make you lightheaded or tingly. Stay seated or lying down; never do it standing, driving or in water. There's no target for the hold: breathe in as soon as you want to. Skip this mode if you're pregnant or have epilepsy, a heart condition or high blood pressure.

No timer. Stay at one intensity for as long as you like. In a headset, push the thumbstick up or down to change it.

Checking for a VR headset…

Slow, intense color patterns with no flashing or strobing. Skip it if you have photosensitive epilepsy, and stop if you feel nauseous, dizzy or uneasy. The floor ring gives your eyes a fixed reference point, which helps with VR comfort.
